PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING POLICY

Version dated 3 October 2026.

1. General Provisions

This Personal Data Processing Policy (the “Policy”) establishes the procedure for processing and protecting the personal data of users of the iroxtech.ru website.

Personal Data Operator:

An individual, website iroxtech.ru

E-mail: hello@iroxtech.ru

Website: iroxtech.ru

This Policy has been prepared in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” and other applicable laws and regulations of the Russian Federation.

This Policy applies to personal data obtained by the Operator through the Website.

2. Basic Principles of Processing

The Operator processes personal data lawfully and fairly.

Processing is limited to achieving specific, predetermined and lawful purposes.

Personal data shall not be processed in a manner incompatible with the purposes for which it was collected.

The scope and nature of the personal data processed shall correspond to the stated purposes of processing.

The Operator takes necessary legal, organizational and technical measures to protect personal data.

3. Categories of Data Subjects

The Operator may process personal data relating to:

  • — visitors and users of the Website;
  • — individuals submitting inquiries through the Website;
  • — representatives and employees of prospective customers, partners and counterparties who voluntarily provide their personal data through the Website.

4. Purposes and Categories of Personal Data

When using the “Send us a video of your operation” form, personal data is processed for:

  • — receiving and reviewing an inquiry;
  • — contacting the user;
  • — analyzing the manufacturing, warehouse or logistics operation described or demonstrated by the user;
  • — carrying out a preliminary assessment of the possibility of robotization and/or automation;
  • — determining a potentially suitable type of solution;
  • — obtaining and clarifying information necessary for the assessment;
  • — preparing and sending information requested by the user;
  • — further communication initiated by the user.

For these purposes, the Operator may process:

  • — name;
  • — company name and place-of-work information;
  • — telephone number;
  • — Telegram username or identifier;
  • — e-mail address;
  • — inquiry text and description of the operation;
  • — information contained in a voluntarily uploaded video;
  • — other information voluntarily provided by the user in the inquiry.

5. Legal Grounds for Processing

The Operator processes personal data where a legal basis provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation exists.

When a form is submitted through the Website, such legal basis includes, in particular, the data subject’s consent to the processing of personal data.

Where provided by applicable law, processing may also be carried out on other lawful grounds.

6. Processing Procedures and Methods

The Operator may perform the following operations:

collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, provision where permitted by law, blocking, deletion and destruction of personal data.

Processing may be carried out using automated means or without the use of automated means.

The Operator does not disclose personal data to third parties without a lawful basis.

For the technical operation of the Website, information systems, e-mail, file storage and processing of inquiries, the Operator may engage third parties and entrust them with the processing of personal data subject to compliance with applicable personal data legislation.

7. Video Materials

A user may voluntarily provide a video of a manufacturing, warehouse or logistics operation.

Such materials are used to analyze the relevant operation and assess the possibility of its robotization and/or automation.

Where a video contains personal data of third parties, the user submitting the materials must have a lawful basis for providing such data to the Operator.

The Operator does not use images of individuals or recordings of their voices contained in such materials for the purpose of identifying those individuals unless a separate lawful basis exists.

8. Retention and Termination of Processing

Personal data shall not be retained for longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed unless another retention period is required by the legislation of the Russian Federation or an applicable agreement.

Upon achievement of the purposes of processing, withdrawal of consent or occurrence of other grounds established by law, the Operator shall cease processing and delete or destroy personal data in accordance with applicable requirements unless there is a lawful basis for continued processing.

9. Personal Data Protection

The Operator takes necessary and sufficient legal, organizational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorized or accidental access, destruction, alteration, blocking, copying, provision, distribution and other unlawful actions.

Access to personal data is provided only to the extent necessary to perform the relevant functions.

10. Rights of Data Subjects

A data subject has the right to obtain information concerning the processing of their personal data as provided by applicable law, request correction, blocking or destruction of personal data where legally permitted, and withdraw previously given consent.

Requests concerning personal data processing may be sent to:

hello@iroxtech.ru

11. Updates to this Policy

The Operator may update this Policy where the personal data processing procedures, services used by the Website or applicable legal requirements change.

The current version of this Policy is available at:
