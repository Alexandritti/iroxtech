CONSENT TO THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

Version dated 3 October 2026.

I, as a user of the iroxtech.ru website (the “Website”), hereby freely, of my own will and in my own interest, give my specific, informed, conscious and unambiguous consent to the personal data operator of the iroxtech.ru website, e-mail: hello@iroxtech.ru (the “Operator”), to process my personal data under the following terms.

1. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • — receiving and reviewing my inquiry;
  • — contacting me regarding my inquiry;
  • — carrying out a preliminary assessment of the possibility of robotizing and/or automating the manufacturing, warehouse or logistics operation described or demonstrated by me;
  • — determining a potentially suitable type of robotic solution;
  • — requesting and clarifying the information required for such assessment;
  • — preparing and sending me a preliminary assessment, proposal or other information related to my inquiry;
  • — organizing further communication and negotiations initiated by me.

This Consent does not provide for the use of my contact details for advertising or marketing communications unrelated to the inquiry submitted by me.

2. Personal Data

This Consent applies to the following personal data that I voluntarily provide through the Website:

  • — name;
  • — company name and information about my place of work, if provided;
  • — telephone number;
  • — Telegram username or identifier, if provided;
  • — e-mail address;
  • — information contained in the description of the operation;
  • — other personal data voluntarily included by me in my inquiry;
  • — personal data that may be contained in a video file voluntarily uploaded by me.

3. Uploaded Video Files

I understand that a video file submitted by me may contain images of individuals, voices or other information relating to identifiable individuals.

I confirm that where materials submitted by me contain personal data of third parties, I have the necessary lawful grounds to provide such materials to the Operator.

The Operator uses uploaded video files for the purpose of analyzing a manufacturing, warehouse or logistics operation and preparing a preliminary assessment of the possibility of its robotization and/or automation.

The Operator does not process images of individuals or voice recordings contained in such videos for the purpose of identifying a person unless this has been separately agreed with the relevant data subject or is otherwise permitted by applicable law.

4. Personal Data Processing Operations

I consent to the Operator carrying out the following operations with my personal data:

collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, updating and modification, retrieval, use, provision to persons engaged by the Operator to technically support the Website and process the inquiry, blocking, deletion and destruction.

Personal data may be processed using automated means or without the use of automated means.

5. Data Transfers and Processing by Service Providers

The Operator may engage third parties to process personal data where such parties provide services necessary for the operation of the Website, hosting, information systems, e-mail communications, file storage and processing of inquiries.

Such processing shall be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose and shall be carried out subject to the requirements of applicable personal data legislation.

Personal data may otherwise be transferred to third parties only where a lawful basis for such transfer exists.

6. Processing Period

Personal data shall be processed until the purposes for which it was collected have been achieved or until this Consent is withdrawn, unless the Operator has another lawful basis to continue processing under applicable legislation.

Upon achievement of the purposes of processing, withdrawal of Consent or occurrence of another applicable legal ground, the processing shall be terminated and personal data shall be deleted, destroyed or anonymized in accordance with the procedure and time limits established by applicable law.

7. Withdrawal of Consent

I may withdraw this Consent by sending a request to the Operator at:

hello@iroxtech.ru

The request should contain sufficient information to enable the Operator to identify the personal data and inquiry to which the withdrawal relates.

Following receipt of the withdrawal, the Operator shall cease processing and delete or destroy the relevant personal data in accordance with applicable law unless there is a lawful basis for continuing the processing without my consent.

8. Confirmation of Consent

By actively selecting the relevant checkbox on the Website and submitting the form, I confirm that I have read this Consent, understand its contents and consent to the processing of my personal data under the terms set out herein.
